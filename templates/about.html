about logs projects

My name is Diego. I'm an aspiring data scientist and machine learning researcher focused on extracting structure and signal from complex, high‑dimensional datasets. My work spans speech‑based biomarkers, genomics, and broader health‑related modeling. I’ve built pipelines for feature extraction, experimental design, and reproducible analysis, and I’m especially interested in how ML can support scientific discovery by surfacing insights that aren’t obvious to the human eye.

Across all these domains, what motivates me is the intersection of applied research and practical engineering. I like understanding how information flows through a system, how models behave under different assumptions, and how to design workflows that scale from exploratory experiments to production‑ready analysis. This site is where I document the tools I’m building, the experiments I’m running, and the ideas I’m exploring as I go deeper into machine learning, data workflows, and evidence‑driven research.
